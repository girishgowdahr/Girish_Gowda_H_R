# Asset Restrictions & Copyright Compliance

> **STRICT COMPLIANCE MANDATE:**
> Absolutely NO proprietary, trademarked, or copyrighted third-party assets are permitted in this project.
> 
> Specifically prohibited:
> - No Garena / Free Fire logos, characters, models, maps, UI skins, audio effects, or animations.
> - No pirated or unlicensed 3D meshes, textures, or audio clips.

## Compliance Requirements
1. Every asset added to `assets-source/` or `frontend/public/assets/` MUST have an associated license record in `assets-source/avatar/LICENSES/` or `assets-source/scenes/LICENSES/`.
2. Asset sources must be either:
   - Created originally by the owner/team, OR
   - Sourced from permissively licensed, verified open repositories with full attribution recorded in `assets.json`.
